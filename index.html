Vancouver, Canada

I’m a computer science PhD in Human-Computer Interaction and data visualization, at SFU.

My research, supervised by Prof. Sheelagh CarpendaleProf. Sheelagh CarpendaleSheelagh Carpendale is a professor of Computing Science at Simon Fraser University and a Canada Research Chair in Information Visualization. Her research spans visualization, interaction design, and human–computer interaction, exploring how people understand and interact with data.Sheelagh's website, challenged the assumption that step-by-step guidance is the best way to onboard users. Instead, I studied how people naturally make sense of unfamiliar interfaces, and I design interaction paradigms that support their learning behaviour.

In my thesis, I introduced serendipitous explanations which is a new approach that provides embedded, in-situ visual explanations as users interact with interface elements, rather than guiding them through a fixed, predefined path. My contributions have been published in leading venues, including IEEE TVCG, ACM PACM HCI, and EuroVis.

Recently, I interned at Autodesk, where I visualized uncertainty in AI-generated output to support more informed, uncertainty-aware decision-making during AI-assisted workflows.
